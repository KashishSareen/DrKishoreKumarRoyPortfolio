About the Surgeon

About Doctor

Trained where India's toughest cancers are treated.

Dr. Kishore Kumar Roy is a Surgical Oncologist — MBBS, MS, FMAS, with a Fellowship in Surgical Oncology from Tata Medical Center, Kolkata. A graduate of The Medical College, Kolkata, Asia's oldest medical college, he has led surgical oncology units at some of India's most respected cancer hospitals across Bengal, Bihar, Haryana and Gujarat.

He now leads Cancer Clinic Kolkata, built to bring surgery, chemotherapy and radiotherapy guidance together, at an affordable cost, for the people of Bengal, Bihar and North-East India.

The Career Path

A Journey Across India's
Cancer Institutions

Foundation

The Medical College, Kolkata & R.G. Kar Medical College

MBBS from Asia's oldest medical college, followed by a critical-care house physician posting and six months in Paediatric Surgery — deliberate preparation for the critical care that major cancer operations demand. MS in General Surgery followed at R.G. Kar Medical College and Hospital, one of Eastern India's busiest surgical units.

Fellowship

Tata Medical Center, Kolkata

Selected for the Fellowship in Surgical Oncology just before completing his residency. Trained across OPD, IPD and Operation Theatre in every sub-speciality of surgical oncology under senior UK-trained faculty, and in peri-operative fluid management as a research protocol in cancer ovary patients.

Rajkot, Gujarat

Consultant Surgical Oncologist, Sahyog Hospital

Began independent consultant practice in a region with among the highest rates of head & neck and oral cavity cancer in India, followed closely by GI cancers.

Hisar, Haryana

Chief Consultant Surgical Oncologist, Sarvodaya Cancer Hospital & Research Institute

Worked alongside Rajasthan's senior-most Medical Oncologists, serving patients from Punjab, Haryana and Rajasthan at the first NABH-accredited cancer hospital in the region after NCR.

Panipat, Haryana

Senior Consultant, Dr. Prem Cancer & Superspeciality Hospital

Invited to help develop a new cancer hospital from the ground up as Consultant Surgical Oncologist.

Patna, Bihar

Unit Head, Mahavir Cancer Sansthan

India's second-largest cancer hospital after TMH Mumbai, seeing over 2,000 OPD patients with 650 dedicated beds. Led the GI, HPB & Thoracic Oncology unit for eight months, then headed Breast, Soft Tissue & Ortho Oncology — performing ultra-radical surgeries and training DNB Surgical Oncology students in reconstruction.

Today — Kolkata, West Bengal

Founder, Cancer Clinic Kolkata

Returned to the motherland to build an economical, comprehensive cancer care project for the poor and middle-class families of Bengal, Bihar and North-East India — the road map to a larger dream of a comprehensive modern cancer hospital.

Surgical Expertise

Every major cancer surgery, one surgeon.

Across a career of thousands of major radical and ultra-radical procedures, Dr. Roy has operated across nearly every organ system affected by cancer — with documented mortality and morbidity under 1%.

Head & Neck Cancer

Commando surgery, total laryngo-pharyngectomy with reconstruction, radical neck dissection, total thyroidectomy, radical parotidectomy.

GI & HPB Cancer

Whipple's procedure, distal pancreatectomy with multivisceral resection, hepatic trisegmentectomy, liver segmental resections, radical cholecystectomy, radical D2 gastrectomy, 2- and 3-field esophagectomy, colo-rectal & retroperitoneal surgery.

Thoracic Cancer

Lung lobectomy, pneumonectomy, chest wall tumour resection & reconstruction, thymectomy, and other mediastinal tumour resections.

Breast Cancer

Modified radical mastectomy, breast conservative surgery with Type I & II oncoplastic reconstruction, sentinel lymph node biopsy, image-guided non-palpable resection, immediate & delayed reconstruction.

Gynaecological Cancer

Ovarian cancer primary & interval cytoreduction, primary peritoneal carcinoma surgery with HIPEC, radical hysterectomy, staging laparotomy, laparoscopic radical hysterectomy.

Urological Cancer

Radical cystectomy with ileal conduit, radical & partial nephrectomy, radical nephrectomy with IVC thrombectomy, radical orchidectomy, penile amputation & reconstruction, groin nodal dissection.

Soft Tissue Tumour & Sarcoma

Wide excision of soft tissue tumours and sarcomas with loco-regional flap reconstruction.

Bone Sarcoma Management

Comprehensive management of bone sarcomas as part of a multi-disciplinary oncology team.

The Practice Philosophy

Care. Education. Knowledge.

Care

A cancer diagnosis is frightening for a patient and their family alike. Every consultation is built around honest, compassionate counselling — especially for patients who feel demotivated or lost after diagnosis.

Education

Patients and families are guided clearly through every stage — diagnosis, surgery, chemotherapy and radiotherapy — so that no one wastes valuable time confused about their treatment path.

Knowledge

Built on fellowship training at Tata Medical Center and years leading surgical oncology units, decisions are grounded in evidence-based, multi-disciplinary cancer management.
